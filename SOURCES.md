## Policy for new material

When adding a third-party figure, dataset, or video thumbnail:

1. Record creator, source URL, and license (or “link-only thumbnail”) in this
   file or in a caption next to the asset.
2. Prefer openly licensed originals; do not strip existing attribution.
3. Keep YouTube and similar media as outbound links rather than rehosting the
   full video.
